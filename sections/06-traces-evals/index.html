Section 06

Make observability part of the value story

Target timing

5 to 6 minutes

Goal: show that teams can inspect behavior and improve quality intentionally.

This is the enterprise credibility section. It shows that an agent is not a black box once it is deployed.

Two layers

Traces explain. Evaluation measures.

Traces

Use traces to answer: what happened on this run, what tool was called, and where did the answer come from?

Evaluation

Use evaluation to answer: is the agent producing results that are accurate, useful, and improving over time?

Operational loop

The strong story here is observe, adjust, and rerun. Avoid discussing evaluation as a one-time checkbox.

Demo risk mitigation

Have at least one known-good trace and one prepared evaluation artifact before the talk starts.

Talking points

Speaker bullets

  • Traces are for understanding behavior at the run level.
  • Evaluation is for understanding quality at the pattern level.
  • This is how teams move from demo success to operational confidence.

Short script

Presenter-ready wording

The part of the platform that tends to win over serious teams is not the first answer. It is what happens after that answer. Can I inspect the run? Can I see how the response was assembled? Can I evaluate behavior over time instead of relying on intuition? Traces and evaluation are the bridge from experimentation to disciplined improvement.

Live switch

What to show in the UI

  • Open Traces and walk through one clear example.
  • Show Evaluation at a high level and emphasize improvement loops rather than test-case mechanics.
  • If the live artifact is noisy, fall back to the principle: traces explain why, evaluation shows whether it is getting better.

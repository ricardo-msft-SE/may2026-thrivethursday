Section 05

Show safety without turning the talk into governance training

Target timing

3 minutes

Goal: answer the enterprise trust question, then move on.

This section is about confidence and control, not deep policy configuration.

Why it matters

Three business answers

Boundaries

Guardrails help teams define where the agent should stay and where it should stop.

Safety

Use this to answer the question: what prevents unsafe or off-policy responses?

Confidence

Executives and architects both want to know the platform has built-in controls, not just a clever demo.

Talking points

Speaker bullets

  • Most customers ask about control almost as quickly as they ask about capability.
  • Guardrails are part of the design, not an afterthought.
  • For this intro, the message is that safe operation is a platform concern, not just a prompt-writing trick.

Short script

Presenter-ready wording

Whenever we talk about AI agents with customers, the conversation quickly shifts from what the system can do to how it can be controlled. Foundry treats that as a platform-level concern. For today, I do not need to dive into every configuration detail. I just want you to see that safety and control are built into the operating model.

Live switch

What to show in the UI

  • Open Guardrails and point out the control surface at a high level.
  • Keep the explanation to one or two examples, then move forward.
  • Tell the audience the next question is not “can we control it?” but “how do we inspect and improve it?”
